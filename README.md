# Rotten-Apple

#### THIS TWEAK IS DANGEROUSLY HARMFUL AND COULD POTENTIALLY BOOTLOOPS YOUR IDEVICES IF YOU DON'T KNOW WHAT YOU'RE DOING

I'm NOT responsible for your job applications from getting deleted in your iDevices.


Rotten Apple is a simple rootless tweak that saves your disabled daemon from jailbroken state to stock iOS state, just like what the title says _"Rotten Apple"_ Once your Apple gets too Rotten, you can't recover it so.. Make a backup before proceeding with whatever you're doing!

To revert everything back, just simply uninstall the tweak and make sure to remove /var/db/com.apple.xpc.launchd/disabled.plist so those disabled daemon won't persist upon reboot or whilst in stock iOS :-)

Tested on iPhone 8, iOS 16.7.15 with Dopamine 2.5b2, Disabled daemon thru iCleaner Pro
